What We Do

We are a non-profit group that works with young people aged 12 to 25. We run after-school classes, sports clubs, and job training to help them grow into confident adults.

Our Work in Numbers

  • 2,500+ young people helped since 2012
  • 18 weekly programmes running
  • 350+ active volunteers
  • 85% of trainees find jobs within 6 months
