About Our Centre

How We Started

Bright Future Youth Centre opened its doors in 2015 in makhado louis trichardt, Gauteng. A group of teachers and social workers saw that many young people in the area had nowhere safe to go after school. They started with one room, a few old computers, and a dream to help.

Today, we run three centres and work with over 2,400 young people every year. We have grown from a small after-school club into a full youth support organisation.

Our Mission

To give every young person the tools, support, and chances they need to build a bright and successful future.

Our Vision

A South Africa where no young person is left behind because of where they live or how much money their family has.

What We Believe In

  • Respect: Every young person deserves to be heard and valued
  • Opportunity: Everyone should have a fair chance to succeed
  • Community: Working together makes us stronger
  • Growth: Learning never stops
