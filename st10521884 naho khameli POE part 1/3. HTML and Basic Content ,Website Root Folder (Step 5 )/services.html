Our Programmes

We run a wide range of free programmes to help young people learn, grow, and prepare for their future.

After-School Homework Help

Qualified tutors help learners with Maths, Science, and English from Monday to Thursday. We provide a quiet space, study materials, and one-on-one support.

Computer Skills Classes

Free classes in Microsoft Office, internet safety, and basic coding. Each student gets hands-on time with a computer and earns a certificate when they finish.

Sports and Fitness

We run soccer, netball, and athletics clubs three times a week. Sports keep young people active, build teamwork, and teach discipline.

Job Readiness Training

Young adults learn how to write CVs, prepare for interviews, and behave in a workplace. We also connect them with local employers for job placements.

Arts and Music

Our arts programme includes drama, painting, and music lessons. Young people can express themselves and discover hidden talents.

Counselling and Support

Trained counsellors offer free, private talks for young people dealing with stress, family problems, or personal struggles. No appointment needed.
